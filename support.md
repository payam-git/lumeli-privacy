## Screen Time & App Blocking

Lumeli uses Apple's Screen Time technologies to provide app restriction features. Screen Time permission must be enabled for Lumeli's app-blocking features to work.

If app blocking is not working as expected, check that Lumeli still has Screen Time authorization in your iPhone settings.
